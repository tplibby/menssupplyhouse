# Direction v3: sleek, image-led (reference: brigadeoverland.com)

Decided 2026-11-06. This replaces the warehouse/parts-bin look (`brand-brief.md`).
The story, voice, verdict system (KEEP/RETURN/DEPENDS) and honesty rules stay.

## Taken from the reference (layout patterns, not branding)
- Split hero: solid dark panel with a condensed uppercase headline and a pill button, large photo beside it. On phones the photo sits on top.
- Category rail: 7 rounded image tiles, label top-left, arrow notched into the bottom-right corner. Swipes on phones.
- Hover list: big condensed titles that swap a photo. Used for the latest reviews, each with its call.
- Full-bleed story band, dark footer.
- Generous whitespace, rounded image corners (~12-14px), photos do the work.

## User decisions
- Palette: close to the reference. Warm stone ground, deep olive panels, brick-red accent.
  (Overrides the earlier ban on green/earthy tones.) Working values: stone #E4E2DB, paper #EFEDE7,
  ink #1C1C1A, olive #2E3A2C, brick #B23A2B, muted #66655E.
- Category tile images: AI scenes WITH generic, unbranded items.
  Guardrails: no logos or recognizable models; used only on category navigation, never on review pages;
  no people or hands.
- Headline font: choosing between Archivo ExtraCondensed / Special Gothic Condensed One / Big Shoulders Display.
  Body: Geist.
