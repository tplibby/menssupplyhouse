Every pair gets the same treatment. No fresh-out-of-the-box verdicts. [SAMPLE]

## Days 1 to 7: break-in

I wear them for full work days, not around the house. I note where they rub and how long it takes to stop.

## Days 8 to 30: real use

Wet grass, concrete, ladders, the drive in. I check the heel, the stitching at the flex point, and the lace hooks.

## What makes me call it

- **KEEP:** comfortable by day 14 and nothing coming apart.
- **RETURN:** still hurts after two weeks, or something fails early.
- **DEPENDS:** great for one kind of foot or job. The review says which.

> If a boot isn't comfortable by day 14, it isn't going to be.

The clock keeps running after the verdict. If something fails at day 90, the review gets an update.
